Lumea Residences at Dubai Islands by Green Horizon Developments

Waterfront residences with premium finishes, refined layouts, and skyline views of Dubai

About

Exclusive collection of 63 premium 1–3 bedroom residences by Green Horizon Developments

Lumea Residences is a low-rise waterfront development on Dubai Islands, offering a limited collection of just 63 refined apartments. The project features thoughtfully designed 1–3 bedroom layouts ranging from 774 to 2,501 sq.ft., with options for storage rooms, spacious terraces, and private Jacuzzis in select units. Each residence maximises natural light through open-plan living spaces, full-height glazing, and expansive balconies with panoramic views of the sea, community, and Dubai skyline. The interiors emphasise functionality, privacy, and flexibility — creating comfortable living environments tailored to modern lifestyles.

Limited Collection — Privacy and Exclusivity

With only 63 units, Lumea offers an intimate scale rarely found in Dubai’s waterfront market. The limited inventory ensures privacy, quiet surroundings, and an elevated sense of ownership. Each floor plan is designed with generous proportions and efficient circulation, appealing to both end-users and investors seeking long-term value in a low-density setting.
